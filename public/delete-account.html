01

Account Deletion

Trimlyhub respects your right to request deletion of your customer account and associated personal information.

Customer accounts can currently be deleted by sending a deletion request to our support email. The customer account does not currently include an in-app Delete Account button.

02

How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of your Trimlyhub customer account:

  1. Send an email to trimlyhub@gmail.com from your registered email address.
  2. Use the subject line Delete My Trimlyhub Account.
  3. Clearly state that you want your Trimlyhub customer account deleted.
Account Deletion Email

trimlyhub@gmail.com

03

Information to Include

For faster processing, please include:

  • Your name
  • Your registered email address
  • Your registered phone number, if applicable
Security Notice

Never send your password, OTP, PIN, CVV, or complete payment-card details by email.

04

What We Delete

After verifying your request, Trimlyhub will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize customer account information that is no longer required for providing our services, security, legal, or business obligations.

  • Customer profile information
  • Account-related personal information
  • Saved account preferences where applicable
  • Other personal information that is eligible for deletion
05

Information We May Retain

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period when required for legal compliance, financial records, refunds, disputes, fraud prevention, security, or other legitimate obligations.

Where retention is required, the information will be kept only for the period reasonably necessary for that purpose.

06

Processing of Your Request

We may verify your identity or account ownership before processing a deletion request. This helps protect accounts from unauthorized deletion requests.

After verification, we will process the request within a reasonable period. Information stored in backups may remain temporarily until those backups are securely overwritten or deleted.

07

Need Help?

If you have questions about account deletion or your personal information, contact us:

Trimlyhub Support

Email: trimlyhub@gmail.com

Support: 23 Hours Daily

We will help you with the account deletion process and any related questions.
