Privacy at Trimlyhub

Trimlyhub respects your privacy and is committed to handling your information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, why we collect it, how we use it, when it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

By accessing or using Trimlyhub, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the platform.

1 Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Trimlyhub, we may collect different categories of information. We aim to collect information that is reasonably necessary to operate, secure, improve, and provide our services.

  • Information you provide when creating or managing an account.
  • Information related to bookings and appointments.
  • Information provided by barber shops and salons when creating their business profiles.
  • Location information where required for location-based features.
  • Payment and transaction-related information.
  • Photos, reviews, ratings, and other content you choose to submit.
  • Device, browser, and technical information.

2 Account Information

When you create or use a Trimlyhub account, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, password or authentication information, and account preferences.

We use this information to create and maintain your account, authenticate you, communicate with you, and provide requested services.

3 Customer Information

If you use Trimlyhub as a customer, we may collect information necessary to facilitate your bookings and interactions with barber shops and salons.

  • Name and contact details.
  • Booking and appointment details.
  • Selected barber, salon, service, date, and time.
  • Booking status and cancellation details.
  • Reviews and ratings you submit.

4 Barber & Salon Information

If you register as a barber, salon, or other service provider, we may collect additional business information required to create, verify, display, and manage your professional profile.

  • Business or shop name.
  • Owner or representative name.
  • Phone number and email address.
  • Business address and location.
  • Services and prices.
  • Shop or profile photographs.
  • Account, booking, and transaction information.

Provider information may be reviewed before a business profile becomes publicly available on Trimlyhub.

5 Location Information

Trimlyhub may use location-related information to provide location-based features, such as helping customers discover nearby barber shops and salons.

Depending on the feature and device settings, location information may come from information you provide directly or from your device, browser, or operating system.

You can generally control location permissions through your device or browser settings. Some location-based features may not work correctly if location access is disabled.

6 Booking & Appointment Information

When you make or manage a booking, we may process information such as the selected service provider, service, appointment date and time, booking identifier, price, booking status, cancellation information, and other information necessary to complete the transaction.

Booking information may be shared with the relevant barber or salon to allow them to provide the service you requested.

7 Payment Information

Trimlyhub may support online payments for bookings and other services. Payment transactions may be processed through third-party payment processors.

Depending on the payment method, payment providers may process information such as transaction identifiers, payment status, payment method details, and other information required to authorize and complete a payment.

Trimlyhub does not ask you to provide your card PIN, CVV, OTP, password, or banking credentials through our customer support forms. Never share such information with anyone claiming to represent Trimlyhub.

8 Photos & User Content

Users and service providers may be able to upload photographs, profile images, shop images, descriptions, and other content.

You are responsible for ensuring that content you upload does not violate another person's privacy, intellectual property rights, or applicable law.

By submitting content to Trimlyhub, you grant us the permissions reasonably necessary to host, display, process, and operate the platform using that content, subject to applicable law and our Terms of Use.

9 Reviews & Ratings

Customers may be able to submit ratings, reviews, feedback, photographs, or other content regarding their experience with a barber or salon.

Reviews may be displayed publicly or associated with a service provider profile. We may moderate, remove, restrict, or decline to publish content that violates our policies, applicable law, or platform standards.

10 Device & Technical Information

When you access Trimlyhub, certain technical information may be automatically collected. This may include:

  • IP address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device type and operating system.
  • Language and general device settings.
  • Pages or features accessed.
  • Date and time of access.
  • Diagnostic, security, and performance information.

This information may be used to maintain security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, understand platform usage, and improve our services.

11 Cookies & Similar Technologies

Trimlyhub may use cookies, local storage, pixels, analytics tools, and similar technologies to keep the platform working, remember preferences, understand usage, and improve user experience.

Some cookies may be provided by third-party service providers used by Trimlyhub.

Your browser may allow you to control or block certain cookies. However, disabling necessary cookies may affect some platform functionality.

12 How We Use Your Information

We may use information collected through Trimlyhub for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Processing and managing bookings.
  • Connecting customers with barber shops and salons.
  • Processing payments and refunds.
  • Providing customer support.
  • Verifying and managing service providers.
  • Sending booking and account-related communications.
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity.
  • Improving platform performance and functionality.
  • Understanding usage trends and analytics.
  • Complying with legal and regulatory requirements.

13 When We Share Information

We do not treat your personal information as something to be sold casually. Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to operate Trimlyhub and provide requested services.

Depending on the situation, information may be shared with:

  • Barbers and salons: Information necessary to fulfill your booking.
  • Payment providers: Information necessary to process transactions and refunds.
  • Technology and hosting providers: Services supporting our website, applications, databases, security, and infrastructure.
  • Analytics and service providers: Information required to understand and improve platform performance.
  • Government or legal authorities: Where disclosure is required or permitted by applicable law.
  • Professional advisers: Where reasonably necessary for legal, accounting, compliance, or business purposes.
  • Business successors: In connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, sale, financing, or transfer of some or all of our business or assets.

14 Information That May Be Public

Certain information may be visible to other users when you use public-facing features of Trimlyhub.

For example, a barber or salon profile may display information such as business name, service list, pricing, business location, photographs, ratings, and reviews.

Please avoid submitting private or sensitive information in fields intended for public display.

15 Data Security

We take reasonable technical, administrative, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no website, application, network, storage system, or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are also responsible for protecting your account credentials and should immediately contact us if you believe your account has been accessed without authorization.

16 Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain legitimate business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, and protect our rights.

The actual retention period may vary depending on the type of information, the purpose for which it was collected, legal requirements, and operational needs.

Subject to applicable law and legitimate business requirements, you may request deletion of your Trimlyhub account and associated personal information.
